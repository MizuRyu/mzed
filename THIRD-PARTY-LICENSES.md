# Third-party licenses

mzed redistributes the following third-party files. They are bundled inside
`assets/`, shipped in the `.app` / `.dmg`, and embedded into the binary for
`mzed serve`. Their licenses require the copyright notice and permission
notice to travel with the distribution, so they are reproduced in full below.

| Component | Files | License |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [github-markdown-css](https://github.com/sindresorhus/github-markdown-css) | `assets/github-markdown.css`, `assets/github-markdown-dark.css` | MIT |
| [highlight.js](https://github.com/highlightjs/highlight.js) | `assets/highlight.min.js`, `assets/highlight-github.css`, `assets/highlight-github-dark.css` | BSD-3-Clause |
| [Mermaid](https://github.com/mermaid-js/mermaid) | `assets/mermaid.min.js` | MIT |
| [KaTeX](https://github.com/KaTeX/KaTeX) | `assets/katex/**` (JS, CSS, WOFF2 fonts) | MIT |

Rust dependencies are linked into the binary rather than redistributed as
source; their licenses (predominantly MIT and Apache-2.0) can be enumerated
with `cargo tree` or `cargo about`.
